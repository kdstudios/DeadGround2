How to play

Expeditions

  • Tap a square to walk there and uncover it. Numbers count the hidden hazards (mines, zombies, SWAT-proof traps) in the 8 squares around them.
  • Hold a covered square, then slide up to reveal or down to flag. Drag to look around the map.
  • Zombies need ammo. Armoured SWAT zombies patrol and only explosives stop them. Planks need a crowbar, locked doors a key, cracked walls an explosive.
  • Mines hurt but never end the run on their own. Shields absorb hits, medkits heal, maps reveal fog.
  • Walk to the open manhole to escape. Loot only counts if you get out.
  • Sealed vault rooms hold extra loot. A lock or terminal minigame opens the door: one attempt only, and failing jams it for good. Always optional.

Safehouse

  • Each day you have stamina: Safe costs 1, Scouting 2, Risky 3.
  • Rescue survivors for passive perks. Everyone eats 1 food per night, and survivors leave if food runs out.
  • Spend scrap and materials on facilities: Storage, Garden, Workshop, Radio, Armoury, Barricades, Watchtower.

Night

  • Zombies march on the safehouse door through the gates. Stand where you like (tap to move), tap a damaged gate to repair it, and tap a tower to choose its archer.
  • Runners are fast but weak, brutes are slow and tough. Losing a night costs some supplies, never the save.

Quick Play

A one-off expedition in a random region. Nothing is saved and your safehouse isn't touched, so winning or dying means nothing.
